Pillar 01

Full-Spectrum BIR Corporate Filings

Comprehensive handling of both monthly withholdings and quarterly/annual tax returns via authorized BIR electronic portals (eFPS / eBIRForms).

  • check Monthly Remittances: Form 0619E (Expanded Withholding) & Form 1601C (Compensation Tax).
  • check Quarterly Tax Returns: Form 2550Q (Value-Added Tax), Form 1601EQ with SAWT generation, and Form 1702Q (Quarterly Corporate Income Tax).
  • check Annual Statutory Returns: Form 1702RT / 1702MX with complete SLSP (Summary List of Sales and Purchases) & Alphalist validation.
  • check Creditable Withholding Taxes: Automatic Form 2307 issuance for suppliers and compilation of certificates from clients.

Pillar 02

SEC & Year-End Statutory Reporting

Guaranteed institutional compliance with Securities and Exchange Commission mandates and annual corporate maintenance requirements.

  • check General Information Sheet (GIS): Timely electronic lodgment via SEC eFAST within 30 days of annual stockholders' meetings.
  • check Audited Financial Statements (AFS): Complete working paper schedules curated for external independent CPAs without friction.
  • check SEC Memorandum Circular No. 28 (MC28): Corporate email & official mobile number compliance protocols maintained.
  • check LGU & Business Permit Renewal: Preparation of gross sales declarations and statutory local tax clearances every January.
