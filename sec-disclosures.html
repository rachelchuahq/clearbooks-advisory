General Information Sheet (GIS)

Filed annually within 30 calendar days from the actual Annual Stockholders' or Members' Meeting via SEC eFAST. Includes beneficial ownership disclosures.

Audited Financial Statements (AFS)

Required for corporations with gross sales/receipts or assets meeting statutory thresholds, stamped received by BIR eAFS prior to SEC eFAST upload.
