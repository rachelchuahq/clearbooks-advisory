Privacy Policy & Data Privacy Notice (RA 10173)

Effective Date: November 2026 • Entity: ClearBooks Advisory (“we,” “us,” or “our”)

1. Commitment to Data Privacy

ClearBooks Advisory respects your right to privacy and complies with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). This notice explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal and corporate data submitted through clearbooksadvisory.online and during our advisory engagements.

2. Information We Collect

When you book a consultation, submit an inquiry, or engage our services, we may collect:

  • Contact & Identity Details: Full name, work email address, phone/Viber/WhatsApp number, and job title.
  • Business & Regulatory Information: Registered business name, trade name, entity type (DTI Sole Proprietorship, SEC Corporation/OPC, Partnership), Tax Identification Number (TIN), BIR Certificate of Registration (COR / Form 2303), and general operational details shared during diagnostic calls.
  • Technical Usage Data: Basic browser metadata, IP address, and cookie analytics used solely to maintain website security and performance.

3. Purpose of Processing

We collect and process your data strictly for legitimate business and regulatory purposes:

  • Scheduling and conducting your initial compliance diagnostic or consultation.
  • Assessing your bookkeeping, tax filing, payroll, or SEC/DTI registration needs to prepare an accurate service proposal.
  • Executing contracted accounting, tax compliance, and advisory services.
  • Fulfilling statutory obligations with Philippine regulatory bodies (e.g., BIR, SEC, DTI, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG) upon formal client authorization.

4. Data Sharing & Confidentiality

  • Zero Commercial Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or business information to third parties.
  • Authorized Personnel & Sub-processors: Access to your data is restricted to authorized ClearBooks Advisory personnel, PRC-accredited CPAs, and encrypted cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., secure email, scheduling, and document storage platforms) bound by strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Regulatory Disclosures: We only submit information to government agencies when explicitly authorized by you under an active engagement or when required by lawful court order or Philippine statute.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement organizational, physical, and technical safeguards—including access controls, encrypted storage, and multi-factor authentication—to protect your records against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Consultation records for non-engaging prospects are retained for up to 12 months for follow-up reference and securely deleted thereafter. Active client records are retained in accordance with BIR and statutory record-keeping mandates (typically 10 years under applicable tax rules).

6. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under RA 10173, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal data is processed.
  • Access and request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent at any time.
  • Request rectification of inaccurate or outdated data, or erasure/blocking of data where legally applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

7. Contact Our Data Protection Officer (DPO)

To exercise your data privacy rights or raise security questions, contact us at:
