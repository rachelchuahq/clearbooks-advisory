Privacy Policy & RA 10173 Compliance Note

ClearBooks Advisory processes financial, tax, and corporate records in strict compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and NPC circulars.

1. Lawful Basis & Scope

We collect only the business registration, TIN, financial ledger, and contact information strictly necessary to perform contracted bookkeeping, BIR tax filing, and SEC statutory services.

2. Confidentiality & Access Control

Client records are isolated in encrypted vaults accessible only to your assigned CPA controller and quality-assurance reviewer. We never sell or share client data with third parties.

3. Data Subject Rights & Retention

In alignment with BIR statutory retention rules (10 years for books of accounts), clients may request data export, correction, or deletion of non-statutory records via our Data Protection Officer at dpo@clearbooks.ph.
