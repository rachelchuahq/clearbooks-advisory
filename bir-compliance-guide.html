Statutory Legal Exposure Warning

BIR Late Filing, Failure-to-Pay & Audit Defense Framework

Non-compliance under the National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC) triggers severe statutory interest, mandatory surcharges, and compromise penalties.

25% NIRC Sec. 248

Mandatory Statutory Surcharge

A 25% surcharge on the basic tax due applies instantly for failure to file on time, filing with an unauthorized RDO, or late payment.

Willful Neglect Trigger: Escalates to a non-negotiable 50% surcharge for fraudulent declarations or intentional refusal to file.
12% NIRC Sec. 249

Legal Deficiency Interest

Accrues at 12% legal interest per annum (effective since TRAIN Law RA 10963) assessed from the statutory deadline until full payment.

Compounding Calculation: Evaluated daily against the basic unpaid deficiency tax, excluding surcharge from compounding base.
RMO RMO No. 7-2015

Compromise Penalty Scale

Consensual criminal liability settlements ranging from ₱1,000 to ₱50,000 per violation based on gross annual revenue or tax liability.

Criminal Provision: Failure to pay compromise subjects responsible corporate officers (President/Treasurer) to court prosecution.

gavel REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11976 OVERVIEW

Ease of Paying Taxes (EOPT) Act: 2025 Structural Changes

Crucial shifts enacted to modernize tax administration, simplify compliance tiers, and eliminate bureaucratic friction for Philippine enterprises.

Taxpayer Categorization Sec. 21 Classification

New 4-Tier Classification Architecture

Taxpayers are now segmented according to gross annual sales, unlocking streamlined income tax returns and reduced civil penalties for smaller entities:

Micro < ₱3 Million
Small ₱3M to ₱20M
Medium ₱20M to ₱1B
Large ≥ ₱1 Billion
check_circle Micro & Small enjoy a capped 10% surcharge and 50% reduced interest rates.
Documentation Shift RR No. 7-2024

The Universal Invoice Mandate

Official Receipts (OR) are no longer the primary substantiation for services. The Invoice is now the sole statutory document for claiming input VAT and tax deductibility across both goods and services.

ClearBooks Compliance: We manage stamping of unused Official Receipts as "Supplementary Invoices" before the regulatory sunset deadline.
payments

File and Pay Anywhere

Elimination of Wrong Venue 25% Surcharge

Taxpayers can now submit returns and settle tax payments electronically through any Authorized Agent Bank (AAB), Authorized Tax Software Provider (ATSP), or Revenue Collection Officer (RCO)—regardless of registered Revenue District Office (RDO).

money_off

Expense Deductibility Decoupled

Repeal of Sec. 34(K) Withholding Condition

Expenses are no longer automatically disallowed for income tax purposes merely because withholding tax was not deducted. The withholding tax liability remains enforceable, but legitimate operational deductions are preserved.
