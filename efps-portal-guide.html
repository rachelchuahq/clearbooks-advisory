BIR eFPS, eBIRForms & eAFS Step-by-Step Workflow

How ClearBooks Advisory automates your monthly and quarterly electronic tax filings, Authorized Agent Bank (AAB) settlements, and eAFS digital archiving.

Step 01 • Enrollment

eFPS & Authorized Agent Bank (AAB) Linking

We verify your RDO classification (Large Taxpayers, TAMP, or voluntary eFPS) and coordinate AAB online banking enrollment so tax payments clear without queue delays.

Step 02 • Preparation

Pre-Filing CPA Reconciliation

Every Form 1601-C, 0619-E, 2550Q, and 1702Q is reconciled against your general ledger, SLSP, and Form 2307 credits 5 days before the statutory deadline.

Step 03 • Filing & Payment

Filing Reference Number (FRN) Confirmation

Upon eFPS submission and payment confirmation, we archive the FRN, bank confirmation receipt, and tax return PDF in your encrypted ClearBooks vault.

Step 04 • eAFS

Electronic Audited Financial Statements (eAFS)

Annual ITR attachments, AFS, and SAWT/Form 2307 scans are packaged per BIR eAFS naming conventions and uploaded with Transaction Reference Numbers.
