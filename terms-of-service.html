Terms of Engagement Summary

Effective Date: November 2026 • Transparent scope, client responsibilities, and confidentiality standards governing ClearBooks Advisory.

1. Website Content & No Automatic CPA-Client Relationship

All articles, tax calendars, checklists, and diagnostic estimates published on clearbooksadvisory.online are provided for general informational purposes only and do not constitute formal accounting, tax, or legal advice. Submitting a contact form or attending a complimentary 20-minute diagnostic call does not establish a formal CPA-client or fiduciary relationship.

2. Formal Engagement & Scope of Work

Professional services begin only upon the mutual signing of a formal Engagement Letter / Service Agreement and settlement of the agreed onboarding fee or retainer.

Each Engagement Letter defines the exact scope of deliverables (e.g., monthly bookkeeping, BIR tax filings, catch-up accounting, or SEC registration), filing deadlines, client responsibilities, and fee structure.

3. Client Responsibilities & Accuracy of Records

Timely and accurate tax and regulatory filings depend on the complete, truthful, and prompt submission of source documents (official receipts, sales invoices, bank statements, payroll logs, and prior BIR/SEC records) by the client on or before agreed monthly cutoff dates.

ClearBooks Advisory is not liable for penalties, surcharges, or interest resulting from late document submission, undisclosed transactions, or inaccurate records provided by the client.

4. Confidentiality & Professional Standards

All financial records, credentials, and business information shared during both pre-engagement diagnostics and active retainers are treated with strict confidentiality in accordance with Philippine accountancy ethical standards and RA 10173.

5. Governing Law

These terms and all service engagements are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of the Philippines.
